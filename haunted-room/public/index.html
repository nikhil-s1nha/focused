The loop. The Attendant only moves while nothing is watching it: look at it, or have its camera up. If it reaches you unwatched, you are caught. The ghost is the opposite — it charges while you ignore it, and staring it down drives it back. Cameras and the floodlight drain power; at 0% the lights die and something comes for you in the dark.

VR: trigger on the Begin/Sound panels or the props; B / Y raises the cameras, trigger on the monitor cycles cameras, hold grip for the floodlight, A / X toggles sound.

The response-proxy slider is inverted on purpose: LOW input builds tension, sustained HIGH input brings relief. Smoothing means changes take a few seconds, never instant jumps.

Live stream mode shows WAIT until a publisher sends data. On desktop browsers, “VR NOT SUPPORTED” is expected — flat rendering still works.
