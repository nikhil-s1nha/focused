CONTROLS / FLOW
- Aim at START / PLAY AGAIN and squeeze the index trigger. Pressing elsewhere does not start.
- A (right) or X (left): sound toggle.
- Grip: no forced jumpscare in normal mode.
- Turn your head to look; no scripted camera turns.
- Two-minute round, then trigger to replay. Desktop Try again starts a new round.
- Exiting VR or losing headset visibility resets a running round and clears
  scheduled scares. Start again on return; old attacks are not queued.
- VR readout shows readiness/countdown, controls, and sound/boost state.

CONTENT
Random door selection, floor crawls, accelerating approaches, watchers,
false retreats, rushes, Momo/clown-inspired procedural models, varied synthesized
sound cues, side-entry lunges for ignored doors, independent dimming/flickers,
empty blackouts and door feints. The procedural characters are not exact imported
movie/meme assets. Five lives per round: a lunge from a door you are not watching costs one life.
Watching the attacking door avoids damage; feints do not cost lives. Zero lives
ends the round with GAME OVER. Lives remain visible on desktop and in the VR HUD.
